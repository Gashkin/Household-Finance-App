# Debt statement evidence

Owner-scoped statement history describes existing debt accounts; it never creates cash or activity. Complete cycles reconcile their components and separately compare the signed ledger closing balance. Dated rates, fees, allowances and maturity constrain deterministic repayment illustrations. Canonically linked settlements reduce cash expectations once; purchases remain spending.
