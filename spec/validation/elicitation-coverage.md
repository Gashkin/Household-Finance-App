# Elicitation Coverage Check

## Coverage Assessment
The current Zachman draft covers the main elicitation themes from the interview:
- Motivation: household planning, visibility, and trust
- Domain: accounts, commitments, transactions, planned vs actual, and reporting
- People: primary planner and secondary consumer
- Journeys: planning, reviewing cashflow, importing transactions, and reconciling deviations
- Scope: spreadsheet seed, no unrelated spreadsheets, no tax/event-specific planning in v1

## Gaps
- The exact shape of transaction automation is still uncertain and is not yet represented as a concrete design decision.
- The initial v1 experience for the secondary consumer is still only lightly specified.

## Verdict
Coverage is sufficient to proceed to audit, with a small number of non-blocking gaps.
