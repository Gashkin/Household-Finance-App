## User Types
- Primary planner: myself, who inputs transactions and commitments
- Secondary consumer: my wife, who uses the reporting data to understand what she can spend and to get a high-level cashflow picture

## Primary Need Per User Type
- Primary planner: to manage commitments
- Secondary consumer: to get a view of cashflow and allowable spend

## Data Ownership and Access
The primary planner owns the data. The secondary consumer can view reporting information but does not own the underlying financial data.

## Responsibility and Notification
The primary planner is responsible when something goes wrong and is the one who acts on it.

## External Parties
Bank accounts, as a source of actual transaction data.
