## Problem Being Solved
The system exists to manage our household finances. I need to know what is in my bank accounts and what will be coming out in the future so I can plan accordingly. It also needs to give both me and my wife a clear picture of where we are financially, although my wife regularly finds the current system too complex and inaccessible.

## Current State Without The System
I have this problem as the primary family financial planner, and I developed the spreadsheet to aid me. The application I am planning is intended to replace that spreadsheet.

## Definition of Success
A cleaner way of interacting with household financial information; a satisfied wife getting a simpler, more accessible view that gives her the information she needs; and possibly direct integration with financial accounts to avoid manual input.

## Non-Negotiables
It must not put the complexity on the user. It should be easy to use. It should never forecast generously; it needs to provide a real picture. It should not miss transactions, because that would break trust.

## External Constraints
The main external constraint is integration with bank accounts, potentially for direct account access or transaction import.
