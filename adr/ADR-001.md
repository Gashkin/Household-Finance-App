# ADR-001: Secondary consumer experience
- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-06-28
- Context: The Zachman audit identified the consumer-facing reporting experience as a decision that needed to be made before generation could proceed.
- Decision: Provide a monthly overview experience for the secondary consumer focused on current balance, upcoming commitments, and allowable spend.
- Constraints: The consumer experience remains simple and read-only; it should not become a full planner workflow.
- Rules out: A detailed planner-style dashboard or a highly interactive reporting experience for v1.
- Supersedes: None
