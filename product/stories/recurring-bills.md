# GBD-19 — Recurring bills and dependable cash flow

Design v1 explicitly approved by the owner in this execution before implementation.

Figma: https://www.figma.com/design/g8R1DoABIB1S6xaPmjweoL

Canva: https://www.canva.com/d/buMdifHTaWfEw-U

Open **Plan ahead** as a financial editor. **Bills & income** maintains recurring and one-off expectations; **Upcoming** reviews occurrences and explicit links to approved activity; **Six-month outlook** explains household and individual account risk; **Payroll calendars** controls pay dates. **Starting evidence & saved plan** retains the previous dated/manual forecast and its what-if workflow. Its horizon now permits up to twelve calendar months. Existing plans are read without migration or loss. The two plans are deliberately displayed separately and are never silently added together; do not enter the same obligation in both and interpret their results as one combined forecast.

Create owned EUR cash accounts in **Money position**, and active categories in **Review & reconcile** first. Enter a change reason, name, account, category, positive exact EUR amount, first due date and recurrence. Amounts may be fixed or estimated. Weekly and fortnightly intervals retain their first-date anchor. Monthly dates clamp to month end then return to the original day in a longer month. An income-linked schedule uses a payroll calendar with an offset from -31 to 31 days. Payroll calendars support explicit weekend days, holidays, previous/next working-day rules and individual pay-date exceptions. No external holiday feed or bank connectivity is assumed.

Use **Preview affected occurrences** before saving. Row save changes one schedule; **Save all changed rows** applies the entire batch atomically. An edit or cancellation starts at an explicit date on/after today and cannot rewrite an earlier version. Stable occurrence IDs use schedule ID and nominal date, independently of shifted payroll or override dates. Effective and end boundaries use resolved due dates, so future nominal anchors cannot rewrite past bills. Historical rules, actor/reason audit and explicitly overridden/matched occurrences are retained, including after future cancellation. Cancelling a schedule does not cancel a bank payment. Use **Upcoming** to override a single future occurrence's date/amount or explicitly retime overdue, unreceived income to a future date; overrides are bounded to 62 days from its nominal date. A delayed income-linked salary override shifts unoverridden bills linked to that payroll month; fixed historical matches and independently overridden bills remain unchanged. Conflicting payroll delays fail before any write.

In **Upcoming**, choose an approved booked actual to **Confirm actual match**, or explicitly **Link pending reservation**. An actual must belong to the scheduled owned account/category and have the appropriate sign. A pending debit is reserved once; settlement changes its evidence fingerprint and requires explicit review/approval of the booked actual before matching again. A booked match included in the selected baseline is not debited again. A booked receipt after a historical baseline is applied as an actual fact, including when its date is before today. A used entry cannot match another occurrence. Corrections, voids and relationship changes invalidate stale matches and block a new outlook until the link is reviewed. Unlinking is explicit and audited. Match a planned transfer only to approved reciprocal booked ledger legs on the same date; incomplete legs remain unconfirmed rather than fabricated.

An unmatched past obligation is **Overdue** only when the owned account's current reconciliation covers its due date. Otherwise it is **Data unconfirmed**. Old unmatched expenses are conservatively retained in the first projected day after the baseline; they are not silently dropped after six months. Overdue expected income is excluded rather than assumed received. Reconcile and review actuals, then link them or explicitly correct the plan.

The default outlook spans six calendar months, with a maximum of twelve. The backend reads dated booked cash less pending debit reservations for owned cash accounts. Borrowing limits and debt accounts are excluded. Expected income remains projected, uncertain income is excluded unless the upside option is chosen, and **Cash-only** excludes future income. Daily lows apply expenses before same-day income. The owned household aggregate is not a view of other members' private accounts. Account risk remains visible even with a positive household balance. Paired planned transfers credit/debit their accounts and have zero household effect, including the household daily low. A missing destination retains the source debit and warns. The unsaved comparison previews a transfer and/or delayed expected income, including linked payroll bills, without changing schedules, ledger records, budget allocations or audit. No feature initiates payments or bank transfers.

Export editable CSV, keep the stable ID/revision columns, and edit the provided dates/categories/amount/recurrence fields. CSV updates existing schedules; new schedules use the UI. The exact exported header is required. **Preview CSV updates** returns every row rejection, and **Download complete rejection report** retains it locally. Apply revalidates every row and rejects the entire operation if any revision, permission, amount, identity or category changed. Formula-like text (including leading whitespace) is escaped for export; quoted commas/newlines and original apostrophes round-trip. No spreadsheet formulas are evaluated. Limits: 100 schedules, 20 payroll calendars, 100 versions per schedule/calendar, 1,000 occurrence overrides/matches, 2,000 durable operation receipts, 100 CSV rows / 200,000 characters and 10,000 occurrences in a projection. Existing workspace backup-size admission can reject earlier; rejected changes leave the whole transaction unchanged.

Saved and draft values remain separate after a stale rejection. Reload saved revisions, compare, then discard or explicitly reapply the draft. A lost response does not justify a blind retry: **Recheck last save status** reads its durable operation receipt. Replaying an identical operation ID returns the current state without an additional write; reusing it with different input is rejected. Same-revision concurrent edits admit only one writer. Access loss hides dependent plan figures and clears client drafts/evidence; admins and other editors gain no ownership rights.

Schedules, payroll versions, exceptions, fingerprints and receipts use the existing owner-scoped `COMMITMENTS` record in `ledger_review_records`, already included in version-7 encrypted backups. No schema migration or archive-version bump is introduced. The existing transaction-lifetime mutation guard and backup recoverability check apply. Restart/restore retains schedules, prior plans, reviewed actuals and actor-aware audit. Development, tests and screenshots use synthetic records only; no real-bank or production-readiness claim is made.
