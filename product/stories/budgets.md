# GBD-18 — Budgets and deliberate allocations

Open **Budgets & allocations** as a financial editor. First create reviewed categories in **Review & reconcile**, then save a reusable regular-income template. Choose a date to instantiate a monthly period. Calendar months start on day 1; pay-day periods run from the configured day to that day next month, clamped to month end. The end date is exclusive. Only one owner budget may cover any date, preventing competing allocations from counting the same spending twice.

Each period preserves its original income and category allocations. Edit current allocations, income and optional allocation dates with a reason. Lower income requires explicit reductions within the revised income; higher income may remain unallocated. A saved template change affects new periods only unless the editor explicitly applies it to the selected period or all instantiated periods of that template from the selection forward. Existing period boundaries never change. Originals remain untouched. Each change retains actor, time, reason and before/after evidence. Requests use revision checks; forward application also checks every affected period and rejects newly added periods before any write; reload and reselect a template after a stale-edit rejection.

Amounts use exact EUR cents on the server. Allocations are planning envelopes, not transfers, bank payments or forecasts. Expected income never becomes cash. Variance compares the original allocation with reviewed spending; remaining compares current allocation plus carry with spending. Unallocated income is explicit and optional; the product never automatically recommends or performs a pension contribution.

Actuals use owned accounts and ledger activity dates in the period. Only confirmed booked activity contributes. Transfers, card settlements and reconciliation adjustments are excluded. A confirmed linked refund/reversal reduces the original purchase's category on the refund's activity date, including a later period. The original purchase must be confirmed and categorised. Unreviewed, pending and uncategorised spending stays visible as an unresolved count and warning rather than trusted actuals. Negative linked income reversals reduce confirmed income on their own activity date, including a later period; they are not spending. Salary paid late belongs to its actual booked period, never its anticipated allocation date. Editing an allocation date cannot change actuals or bank balances.

At creation, accumulating categories snapshot the immediately preceding adjacent period's remaining amount, including deficits. Reset categories start without carry. Carry is separate from new-income allocations. Later corrections never silently rewrite the snapshot: the next period warns when predecessor allocations or actuals change. Use **Update carried balances from previous period** to adopt the displayed reviewed predecessor amounts with a reason. The server rejects unresolved earlier activity, changed source amounts and stale period revisions. The original carry snapshot stays immutable; a separate current-carry snapshot and its source revision are recorded in history and backup. Current allocations and template application retain that correction. Keep any category with a carried balance in that period. Categories may be renamed/retired without losing original names in history; new templates and new category allocations require active categories. Existing retired allocations remain editable so carried balances cannot trap a period. A nonzero accumulating balance cannot disappear when a new template omits its category: include it, or explicitly reset the previous period category with an audited reason before creating the next period. Category merges remain ledger decisions and do not rewrite saved budget originals.

Download the current JSON budget report for original/current/actual/variance figures and warnings. The report contains private financial planning information and is an intentional local export. No AI, bank or external recipient is involved. Reports are current views; saved budget decisions and audit evidence are durable. Editor plans are private to their creator; Reader/Admin roles gain no extra access to another user's plans or evidence. Existing authenticated sessions, CSRF, loopback and endpoint permissions apply.

Templates and periods use typed `BUDGET_TEMPLATE` and `BUDGET_PERIOD` records in `ledger_review_records`, already included in version-7 encrypted whole-workspace backups. No schema migration or archive-version bump is needed. All mutations use the existing transaction-lifetime workspace admission and recoverability guard. Restoring an encrypted backup restores originals, edits, dates, reviewed actuals and audit. Development and verification use synthetic data only; this feature makes no real-bank release-readiness claim.

Verification: `BudgetTest` covers penny arithmetic, invalid/stale requests with unchanged state, month-end/leap boundaries, overlap, refund/transfer interactions, late corrections after rollover, forward template application, denied ownership, HTTP session/CSRF/types and concurrent edits. `BudgetRecoveryTest` checks file restart and encrypted restore. `frontend/e2e/budget.spec.ts` exercises the complete desktop/mobile journey, stale rejection, history, local report download and horizontal layout.
